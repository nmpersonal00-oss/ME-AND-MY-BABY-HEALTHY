Protocol stopping rule

Stop the observation

Either participant can stop the study. Select a crossed boundary if one occurred, or stop voluntarily. Reporting a boundary is not a judgment about either person; no name or intimate detail is recorded.

You may withdraw consent at any time. Your past private records can still be reviewed and deleted by you afterward.
